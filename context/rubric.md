# Rubric: how to judge a posting

The rubric decides, not the mood of the run. Same posting, same score, every time.

Read `../memory/calibration.md` first. Rules approved there override this file where they conflict.

## Step 1: gates
Check the hard gates in `search-brief.md` before scoring. A posting that fails a gate is RED: record which gate and the quote that shows it, then stop.
If the posting cannot be read on the employer's own board, mark it UNVERIFIED and do not score it.

## Step 2: score five dimensions
Each dimension gets **exactly 1, 3 or 5**. Nothing in between. Tune the weights to what matters to you; they must add up to 100.

| Dimension | Weight | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Level fit | 25 | Asks for roughly your years and level | One band above or below | Two or more bands away |
| Stack fit | 25 | Names the work you have actually shipped | Generic overlap | Mostly tools you have not used |
| Pay fit | 20 | States pay at or above your floor | Silent, at a company known to pay that | Silent and unlikely, or tops out near the floor |
| Transferable strength | 15 | Rewards what makes you different | Rewards some of it | Rewards little of it |
| Access | 15 | A referral or warm contact exists (name it) | Direct application on the employer's portal | Agency, aggregator-only, or unclear route |

**Total** = sum of (weight × score ÷ 5), from 20 to 100.
**Verdict:** 70 or more is GREEN. 50 to 69 is AMBER. Below 50 is RED. A real referral jumps the queue unless a gate failed.

## Step 3: evidence. No quote, no score.
- Every dimension score carries a **verbatim quote from the JD snapshot**, 25 words or fewer.
- Stack fit and Transferable strength also carry **the matching line from `profile.md`, `application-data.md` or `claims.md`**. No matching line means that requirement is missing.
- Pay with a silent JD: write `JD silent` and the reason for the score. Never guess a number.
- Access: the evidence is the route itself (portal URL, or the named contact).
- Skills on the "NOT confirmed" list in `claims.md` never count as a match.

## Step 4: honest gaps
List up to three JD requirements you do not meet, each with its quote. A role under 90 with no gaps listed gets sent back by the verifier.

## Step 5: one line each
Why it fits. Biggest risk. Plain words.
