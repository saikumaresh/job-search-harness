# Ideal role: what strong evidence looks like

## When NOT to copy from this example
This scorecard is **illustrative**, for a fictional candidate. Do not copy the company, the title, the stack words, the quotes or the total. Copy only the **format**: a gate check, one verbatim quote per dimension, the matching profile line, honest gaps even on a strong role, and one plain line each on fit and risk.

---

**Honest gaps**
